
**Step 1: The "Good News" Sandwich**
Start with the positive outcome.
> "First, I wanted to share some good news. I’ve accepted an offer at [Company] as a [Role]. I really appreciate the foundation this program has given me to get there."

**Step 2: The Honest Confession**
Pivot immediately to the reality.
> "However, I need to be honest with you about my current standing in this specific course. The interview process was incredibly intense over the last few weeks, and I made the decision to prioritize those final rounds. Because of that, I have fallen behind on my preparation for your final exam."

**Step 3: The "Respect for Material" Pivot**
This is crucial. You aren't asking for a delay because you are lazy; you are asking because you want to actually learn.
> "I realized yesterday that if I take the exam this week, I will likely fail or perform very poorly. I don't want to just 'get through' this class; I actually want to master this material, especially now that I’ll be working in the field."

**Step 4: The Ask (The Solution)**
Make it easy for them.
> "Would you be open to granting me an Incomplete? I am asking to take the exam in January [suggest a specific date range, e.g., first week of Jan]. This would allow me to study properly over the break and submit work that reflects my actual ability, rather than rushing it now."
